* Bug **#4047** : Fix the types of date arithmetic. A date minus a date and a duration multiplied or divided by a number are now durations rather than numbers, and `round`, `floor` and `ceiling` of a date or duration keep its type. A duration is shown exactly, e.g. `1m1s` or `-12h` rather than `1m` or `-43200000ms`. Adding a duration or period to a blank value gives a blank, as subtracting one does. Adding a date to a date, multiplying a date, or dividing a date by anything but a number, is now an error. So is dividing `1d` or `1w` by a number or multiplying it by a fraction, so use hours such as `24h`. Use `toLong` to work with the milliseconds of a date.
