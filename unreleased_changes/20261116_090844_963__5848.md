* Feature **#5848** : Compact an idle Ask Stroom AI chat before its user comes back: a chat idle for `stroom.ai.compaction.idleAfter` (default 10 minutes) and over `stroom.ai.compaction.idleThresholdPercent` (default 60) of its context budget is compacted by the new job 'AI Chat Idle Compaction', as its owner, with the model the chat last used, and shown as 'Compacted while the chat was idle'. A question sent meanwhile waits for it, and a run's tool calls or thinking count as activity.
