* Feature **#4047** : Add a period type to expressions for amounts of calendar time. `1d`, `2w`, `3M` and `1y` are periods that are added to dates in the time zone of the query, so `EventTime + 1d` keeps its local time when the clocks change. Use `24h` for exactly 24 hours. A period of days or weeks still counts as that many 24 hours where the other value is a duration or a number. Periods of months or years can't be compared with `<` or `>`, or used in `min`, `max` or `inRange`, as their lengths depend on the date. Add the functions `parsePeriod`, `formatPeriod` and `toPeriod`.
