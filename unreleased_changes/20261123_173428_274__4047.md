* Bug **#4047** : Fix duration literals in expressions so that `1w` is accepted and `1M` and `1y` are refused rather than `1M` being read as one minute. Units are now case-sensitive, so `1H` in an expression must be written `1h`, and `1D`, `1W` and `1Y` in a `window` clause must be written `1d`, `1w` and `1y`. Accept `ms` in date expressions.
