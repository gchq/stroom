* Bug **#4047** : Change `floorTime`, `floorDay`, `isWeekend`, `formatDate` and similar functions to read dates as the new calendar functions do. Text with no offset is in the time zone of the query, or that given to `formatDate`. A duration, period, boolean or number that is too large is an error rather than a date in 1970, as is text of a duration. Other text that is not a date is still blank, or an error from `formatDate`. Use `floor`, `ceiling` or `round` to round a duration.
